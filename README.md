## Abstract

Forecasting stock–bond correlations is critical to both portfolio managers and risk managers. The correlation between equities and government bonds fluctuates depending upon the state of the economy. Structured macroeconomic data have been the primary source of forecasting for many years, but these traditional methods do not always reflect the forward-looking nature of the economic narrative contained in policy communications. The purpose of this research is to determine if sentiment analysis of the Federal Reserve’s Beige Books could improve the predictive power of future expected U.S. stock–bond correlation.
